Plans and subscriptions

The free plan includes a limited number of AI meetings each week. The Break Room Fund subscriptions include more, as described in the app at the time you subscribe. Snack Committee is limited to a number of meetings a day. Owns the Microwave has no daily meeting limit, but meetings are paced at up to one every 5 minutes to prevent automated abuse. The Corner Office is a one-time purchase with the same paced, unlimited meetings. It does not renew, and it lasts for as long as Begrudged is offered (“lifetime” means the life of the app, not yours); Restore Purchases brings it to a new device. The expansion offices (The Night Shift, The Permits Office, The Front Office) are optional one-time purchases that unlock meetings at that office; every paid plan includes them while the plan is active. Meetings at an office count toward your plan’s limits like any other.

Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews automatically each month at the price shown unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it in your Apple ID’s Subscriptions settings. Refunds are handled by Apple.

If a meeting can’t be written by the AI (for example when a limit is reached or the service is busy), the office writes a simpler one on your device instead.
