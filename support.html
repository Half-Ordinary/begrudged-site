Support

Begrudged is a comedy app for adults: you describe something that annoyed you, and a fictional office holds a meeting about it.

Contact: support@halfordinary.com

Deleting your data

Open Settings in the app and tap Delete my account. It removes everything the app stored on your phone and erases the daily usage count our server keeps for your install. To remove a single grievance, open Records.

Calendar, reminders and alarms

The app only adds an event or reminder after you tap Approve, and only to the calendar or list you pick. Items you add are yours and stay in those apps if you delete Begrudged. iPhones don't let apps set Clock alarms, so a wake-up becomes a calendar event with an alert.

How are meetings written?

Each meeting is written by an AI model (Claude, from Anthropic). The grievance text, your alias and a short summary of the fictional office are sent through our server to write it. Our server doesn't keep the text. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Reporting a meeting

If a meeting says something offensive or harmful, tap Report this meeting at the end of it, or email support@halfordinary.com. We read every report.

Is this therapy?

No. It's fiction. If you're in danger or need help, contact local emergency services or a crisis line.
