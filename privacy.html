Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Summary

Begrudged has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What you write stays on your device unless AI scenes are on, in which case it is sent only to write that scene.

What stays on your device

Your alias and generated job title, your grievances, the meetings and reports the office writes, your settings, and the actions you approve (calendar events, reminders, alarms). This is stored in the app’s private storage on your device. We cannot see it.

What is sent when AI scenes are on

To write a scene, the app sends the text of the grievance, your alias and title, your humor and language settings, and a short summary of the fictional office to our server, which runs on Supabase. Our server passes it to our AI provider, Anthropic, to generate the scene, and sends the scene back.

We do not store the grievance text or the scene on our server. Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms, which do not allow it to train models on this data. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

You agree to this when you accept the terms on first launch. If you prefer not to send anything, do not file grievances you would not want processed this way.

What our server keeps

A random install number, created by the app, and a count of how many AI scenes that install used each day. We use it only to enforce daily limits that keep costs in check. It contains no name, contact detail or grievance text.

Calendar, reminders, alarms and notifications

The app asks for calendar or reminders access only when you approve adding something. It writes only the items you approve. If you choose to include your calendar in the Morning Memorandum, the app reads upcoming events on your device to list them. This information never leaves your device.

The Morning Memorandum notification is scheduled on your device and only if you turn it on.

Deleting your data

Delete any grievance in Records. Settings › Delete my account removes everything the app stored on your device, turns off its notification, and erases the server’s scene count for your install. Items you added to your calendar or reminders stay there, since they are yours; remove them in those apps.

Age

Begrudged is for adults 18 and over and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app with a new date.
