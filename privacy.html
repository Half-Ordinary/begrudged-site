Privacy Policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

Summary

Begrudged is made by Half Ordinary Software LLC. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What you write stays on your device unless AI scenes are on, in which case it is sent only to write that scene.

What stays on your device

Your alias and generated job title, your grievances, the meetings and reports the office writes, your settings, and the actions you approve (calendar events, reminders, alarms). This is stored in the app’s private storage on your device. We cannot see it.

What is sent when AI scenes are on

To write a scene, the app sends the text of the grievance, your alias and title, your humor and language settings, and a short summary of the fictional office to our server, which runs on Supabase. Our server passes it to our AI provider, Anthropic, to generate the scene, and sends the scene back.

We do not store the grievance text or the scene on our server. Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms, which do not allow it to train models on this data. Anthropic may keep it for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring, then deletes it. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising. You can withdraw this permission at any time in Settings by turning off Send grievances to the AI writer; after that nothing is sent and meetings are written on your device.

You agree to this when you accept the terms on first launch. If you prefer not to send anything, do not file grievances you would not want processed this way.

What our server keeps

A random install number, created by the app, and a count of how many AI scenes that install used each day. We use it only to enforce the meeting limits of your plan and keep costs in check. It contains no name, contact detail or grievance text.

Subscriptions

If you subscribe, Apple handles the payment. We never see your card, name or Apple ID. We use RevenueCat to check that a subscription is active: the app and our server send RevenueCat the same random install number, and RevenueCat receives the App Store’s record of the purchase (product, price, dates and renewal status). We use it only to give you the plan you paid for.

Calendar, reminders, alarms and notifications

The app asks for calendar or reminders access only when you approve adding something. It writes only the items you approve. If you choose to include your calendar in the Morning Memorandum, the app reads upcoming events on your device to list them. This information never leaves your device.

The Morning Memorandum notification is scheduled on your device and only if you turn it on.

Photos and documents you hand over

Under Reception › Paperwork you can take a photo or pick a screenshot of a document, such as a school flyer or a schedule. The app asks for camera access only when you tap Take a photo, and opens your photo library only to let you pick one image. It never browses your photos.

The image you pick is resized on your device and sent to our server, which passes it to Anthropic to read the dates in it, then sends the list of dates back. We do not store the image or the list on our server. Anthropic handles it under the same terms described above. Each date becomes a suggestion you can approve, edit or dismiss; nothing is added on its own. The app asks before the first photo is sent, and you can withdraw that permission in Settings (Let Clive read photos with AI).

Deleting your data

Delete any grievance in Records. Settings › Delete my account removes everything the app stored on your device, turns off its notification, and erases the server’s scene count for your install. Items you added to your calendar or reminders stay there, since they are yours; remove them in those apps.

Age

Begrudged is for adults 18 and over and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app with a new date.
